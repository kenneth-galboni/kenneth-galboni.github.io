## 🔬 Featured R&D Projects

### 🌲 3D HD LiDAR & Urban Vegetation Exposure Metrics (3-30-300 Rule)
* **Context:** SESSTIM Research Lab (AMU, Inserm, IRD) — Marseille, France[cite: 10.2].
* **Overview:** Developed automated R & Python pipelines using high-density 3D LiDAR data and summer NDVI satellite imagery to quantify building-scale green space access and evaluate environmental exposure inequalities[cite: 10.2].
* **Tech Stack:** Python, R (`sf`, `raster`), QGIS, LiDAR Processing[cite: 10.2].
* **Links:** [📄 Data Paper in Prep] | [🔗 Repository](https://github.com/kenneth-galboni/urban-vegetation-lidar)

### 📊 Spatial Profiling & Multivariate Clustering for Public Health Stratification
* **Context:** SESSTIM Research Lab (AMU, Inserm, IRD) — PACA Region, France[cite: 10.2].
* **Overview:** Built and harmonized a high-dimensional spatial database across 2,434 census blocks (IRIS) and 15 satellite-derived vegetation indicators[cite: 10.2]. Implemented dimension reduction (PCA) and unsupervised clustering (HCPC, K-medoids) with internal spatial consistency benchmarking[cite: 10.2].
* **Tech Stack:** R (`FactoMineR`, `tidyverse`), QGIS, Stata[cite: 10.2].
* **Links:** [📄 Manuscript (PLOS ONE sub.)] | [🔗 Repository](https://github.com/kenneth-galboni/spatial-clustering-paca)

### 📈 Bayesian Epidemic Forecasting & Competing Risk Survival Modeling
* **Context:** Centre MURAZ / INSP — Bobo-Dioulasso, Burkina Faso[cite: 10.2].
* **Overview:** Designed and implemented Bayesian SIR models for outbreak forecasting and formalized Jeong & Fine competing risk models with custom prior distributions (Gamma, Jeffreys, MDIP) for health trajectory modeling[cite: 10.2].
* **Tech Stack:** R, Bayesian Statistics (MCMC), Gompertz Distributions[cite: 10.2].
* **Links:** [📄 Publication (Afr J Math Stat Stud)](https://doi.org/10.1002/example) | [🔗 Repository](https://github.com/kenneth-galboni/bayesian-survival-analysis)
